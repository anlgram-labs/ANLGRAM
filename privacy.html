Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. ANLGRAM is dedicated to preserving the anonymity, privacy, and sovereignty of users on The Open Network (TON).

1. Zero Collection of Personal Identifiable Information (PII)

ANLGRAM does not collect, record, or store names, email addresses, physical addresses, phone numbers, or government-issued identification numbers. No account registration or KYC is required to use this decentralized interface.

2. Cryptographic Wallet Keys & Passphrases

ANLGRAM operates strictly as a static, client-side web application. Under no circumstances does ANLGRAM request, view, log, or transmit:

  • Private keys;
  • Mnemonic seed phrases (12 or 24 words);
  • Wallet passwords or PINs.

All transaction authorization is handled entirely within your personal wallet software via standard TON Connect protocols.

3. Blockchain Data & Public Transparency

When you query an address or broadcast a staking transaction, that transaction is published to the public TON blockchain ledger. Public blockchain data (including transaction hashes, account balances, and contract interactions) is immutable, publicly accessible, and outside the control of ANLGRAM.

4. Local Storage Usage

ANLGRAM utilizes your browser's local storage (localStorage) exclusively for client-side user preferences, including:

  • Connected wallet address display preference;
  • Preferred currency display (USD, EUR, etc.);
  • Selected block explorer link preference;
  • Network toggle preference (Mainnet vs Testnet).

This information remains exclusively on your local device and is never transmitted to an external central database.

5. Third-Party RPC Providers & APIs

To retrieve real-time network metrics and pool state, ANLGRAM interacts directly with public nodes and APIs, including TonAPI (tonapi.io) and CoinGecko. These third-party services operate under their respective privacy policies.

6. Contact & Updates

Updates to this Privacy Policy will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. For questions regarding our privacy architecture, consult our official documentation and open-source repositories.
