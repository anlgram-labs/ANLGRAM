Mathematical Formulas Applied
1. Units Purchased = Invested Amount ÷ Buy Price
2. Total Cost = Invested Amount + Buy Fee
3. Gross Sell Value = Units Purchased × Sell Price
4. Net Sell Proceeds = Gross Sell Value − Sell Fee
5. Net Profit / Loss = Net Sell Proceeds − Total Cost
6. ROI (%) = (Net Profit or Loss ÷ Total Cost) × 100
