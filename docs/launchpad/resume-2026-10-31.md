## Implemented development corrections

The component generator previously emitted addresses without independently
compiling the source, omitted serialized StateInit, and still described the
completed V3 lifecycle as incomplete. It now:

- Source-compiles all seven address dependencies and rejects mismatched bytecode,
  recorded cell hashes or BOC digests before generating a candidate.
- Records normalized hashes of every compiler input and compiler version; exports
  canonical complete StateInit for the binding, locker and four dependency roles.
- Exposes validated immutable lock choices/beneficiaries and full uint64 operation
  IDs through its offline function, preserving the existing defaults/recipes.
- Produces two unsigned, operation-correlated query templates with explicit caller,
  destination and minimum funding. Funding minima are existing contract requirements,
  not a current network fee quote or permission to transact.
- Distinguishes locally completed V3 from missing exact LP issuance/withdrawal
  evidence, live code approval and a complete V2 liquidity executor.
- Adds `--check` to reject a stale checked-in candidate reproducibly, without
  writing files or connecting to a network.

from a development ref. Its deployment job is now restricted to `main`. This is
a development-only guard, not a change to production's reviewed packaging workflow.
